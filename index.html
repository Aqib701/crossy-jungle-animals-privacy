This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game "Crossy Jungle Animals" (the "App"), published on Google Play by AllOut Games ("we", "us", "our"), handles information. In short: the App does not collect, share or sell personal information, and it contains no advertising.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or sensitive user data. The App does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and it does not access your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or location.

The App stores game progress (best score, coins, unlocked characters, daily mission progress and settings such as sound and vibration) only on your own device, using the device's local storage. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else.

2. Advertising, analytics and third parties

The App contains no advertisements and does not use advertising identifiers. It does not include third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services, and it does not share any data with third parties.

3. Permissions used

  • INTERNET - standard network access permission used by the Android and Unity runtime. The App does not use it to send your personal information to us or to third parties.
  • VIBRATE - used for short vibration feedback during play. It can be turned off in the game settings.

4. Children

The App is suitable for a general audience, including children. Because it collects no personal information, shows no ads and has no social or chat features, it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address above and we will delete it.

5. Data security

Because game data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security features (for example screen lock and Android's app sandboxing). We do not operate servers that store your information.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not retain any user data. All game data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App changes. The latest version will always be available at this web address, with the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about the App's handling of data, contact AllOut Games at aqibshafique94@gmail.com.
